# NPC behavior wishlist (from the game's creator)

Status: design target. M3 built left-lane campers, tailgaters, trucks and weavers; the rest is still to come.

## Distracted
Texting (lane drift, random slowing, late reactions); eating, makeup or shaving; rubbernecking at crashes, slowing both directions; lost tourists on GPS (sudden lane changes, last-second exits); singing or on FaceTime.

## Aggressive
Road rage (brake-checks, blocking, chasing you down an exit); honking, flipping off, yelling; tailgating and flashing high beams; speeding up so you can't pass or merge; cutting across three lanes for an exit; racing you from a stop or merge.

## Selfish or oblivious
Left-lane campers; side-by-side rolling roadblocks; never signaling or blinker left on for miles; merging at 40 mph onto a 70 mph highway; stopping dead at the end of a ramp; zipper-merge refusers and line cutters; slamming brakes on seeing a cop, even at the limit.

## Dangerous
Drunk or high drivers (weaving, wrong speeds); drowsy drivers on the rumble strip late at night; rare wrong-way drivers; motorcycles lane-splitting or doing wheelies; street racers and car-meet packs.

## Situational
Trucks drafting, or two semis side by side for miles; overloaded pickups dropping mattresses and ladders; rain with no headlights or hazards on while moving; slowing too much in light rain or not at all in heavy rain; construction zones (merges, workers, confused drivers); breakdowns on the shoulder and people changing tires; funeral processions, oversized loads and pilot cars.

## Regional flavor
- Florida: retirees at 50 mph, rental cars braking randomly, huge lifted pickups.
- Georgia: everyone does 85, then slams brakes near a GSP cruiser.
- Virginia: whole highway at exactly the speed limit.
- NJ/NYC: honking at everything, merging by force, no space ever.
- New England: aggressive in snow, tailgating on ice.

## Game twists
Cops hiding in medians and behind overpasses; Good Samaritans flashing lights to warn of speed traps; truckers who let you draft or block cops if you've been driving clean.
